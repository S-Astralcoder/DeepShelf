Your digital bookshelf, powered by meaning.

Never lose a great read again. Save links to books, articles, and text websites, then find them instantly by searching for concepts and ideas—not just exact keywords.

Features

Universal URL Ingestion

Save links to books, articles, research papers, and text websites. The application extracts the core readable content cleanly.

Automated AI Summarization

AI parses and distills long-form web content into concise summaries, capturing the core ideas without the noise.

Summary Vector Storage

AI-generated summaries are converted to embeddings and stored in a vector database for high-level conceptual discovery.

Full-Text Vector Indexing

Complete page contents are chunked and embedded alongside summaries to preserve granular context and specific passages.

Two-Way Semantic Search

Perform dual-layer queries across your library—search broad themes across summaries or retrieve exact excerpts through full-text vectors.

Concept-Based Knowledge Retrieval

Retrieve saved knowledge using natural language and intent, locating relevant reads even when you do not remember exact keywords.
